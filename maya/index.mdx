Maya is your personal AI assistant. She lives in the **Talk to Maya** chat and helps with everyday work: your email, calendar, reminders, files and apps, and research. She is also the queen of your hive. She asks the agents in your swarms for help, and she builds agents and swarms when you ask.

You can ask her anything, from "What's on my calendar tomorrow?" to "Draft a reply to Alex's last email" or "What did my finance swarm produce last week?" She understands your context, remembers what matters, and routes work to the agents and swarms you have created.

## Maya and your apps

Maya gets every app and credential you enable for **All agents**, plus any you attach to her directly. Untick her in an app's or credential's access settings to keep it away from her.

- Maya runs read-only tools, such as reading email or listing events, directly.
- Before a change, such as sending an email or editing a calendar event, she asks you to confirm. On the web, tap **Yes** on the card. In Telegram or Discord, or when you type your reply, answer `1` to approve or `2` to decline. Any other reply does nothing.
- Maya's scheduled jobs can read and report, but they can't send or change data in your apps.
- Maya never asks you to paste an API key in chat. Keys go through the secure credential card or the [Credentials](/integrations/credentials) page.

See [Connect apps](/integrations/connect-apps#how-maya-uses-connected-apps) for details.

## How Maya Remembers

Maya learns from every conversation. She stores facts and experiences in your [memory](/maya/memory), which can be shared across all your agents or kept private per agent. You control what she remembers and how it is used.

## Starting a Conversation

Open the sidebar and click **Talk to Maya**, or select **New Chat** from the chat history panel. Every conversation is stored in your chat history so you can pick up where you left off.
