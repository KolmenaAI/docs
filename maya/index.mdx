Maya is your personal AI agent and the central guide to Kolmena. She lives in the **Talk to Maya** chat and acts as the queen of your hive: she helps you build agents, configure swarms, answer questions, and delegate tasks to the right specialist.

Think of Maya as your on-call coordinator. You can ask her anything, from "Build me a marketing agent" to "What did my finance swarm produce last week?" She understands your context, remembers what matters, and routes work to the agents and swarms you have created.

## Manage chats and apps by asking

You can manage your chat history and connected apps in plain language instead of clicking through settings.

**Chats.** Maya can:

- List your chats. She includes archived chats when you ask for them.
- Rename a chat, pin or unpin it, and archive or unarchive it.
- Delete chats, up to 50 per request. She never deletes the chat you are talking to her in.

**Connected apps.** Maya can:

- List your connected apps and which agents use each one.
- Choose which agents an app applies to, or apply it to all of them.
- Turn an app off for specific agents.
- Limit which of an app's tools an agent may use.
- Disconnect your account from an app, or remove the app from every agent.

If an app or agent name matches more than one item, Maya asks you which one you mean. To disconnect or remove an app, give its exact name as it appears in your app list.

```text
Archive all my chats about the Q3 launch and pin the one called "Budget review".
```

```text
Which agents use Gmail? Turn it off for my Research agent and only let the Sales agent send emails.
```

## How Maya Remembers

Maya learns from every conversation. She stores facts and experiences in your [memory](/maya/memory), which can be shared across all your agents or kept private per agent. You control what she remembers and how it is used.

## Starting a Conversation

Open the sidebar and click **Talk to Maya**, or select **New Chat** from the chat history panel. Every conversation is stored in your chat history so you can pick up where you left off.
