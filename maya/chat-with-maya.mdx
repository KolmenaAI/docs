You can talk to Maya at any time from the Kolmena sidebar. Every conversation is saved to your chat history so you can resume, organize, or clean up past threads without losing context.

## Start a New Chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar">
    Click the sidebar toggle or press the keyboard shortcut to open the left panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Talk to Maya">
    Select **Talk to Maya** to jump into your main chat, or click **New Chat** to start a fresh thread.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your message">
    Type in the composer and press Enter, or use the voice button to speak instead of typing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage Chat History

Your chat history lives in the sidebar under **Chats**. You can organize it to keep important conversations within reach.

| Action | How to do it |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Use the search box at the top of the chat history panel to find conversations by name or content. |
| **Pin** | Hover over a chat row and click the pin icon to keep it in the **Pinned** section at the top. Click again to unpin. |
| **Rename** | Hover over a chat, click the pencil icon, type a new name, and press Enter. |
| **Archive** | Hover over a chat and click the archive icon to remove it from the sidebar. The conversation and its transcript are preserved; you are only hiding the row. |

You can also group chats by date or by agent and swarm, and sort them by recent activity, creation date, name, or a manual order you define.

## Attach Files

You can attach files to any message so Maya or your agents can read, analyze, or transform them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the attachment picker">
    Click the attachment button in the message composer, or drag and drop a file directly into the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the file">
    Choose documents, images, spreadsheets, or other supported files. You can also tag knowledge files from your library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Add an optional message and send. The agent will reference the attachment in its reply.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can open an attachment in a new tab, download it, or view dashboards directly from the chat thread.
</Tip>

## Use Voice Mode

Voice mode lets you talk to Maya hands-free. The spoken turns appear in the chat thread as ordinary messages, so the conversation stays readable and searchable.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the voice button">
    In the composer, tap the microphone or voice icon to start a voice session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Speak naturally">
    Talk to Maya as you would in a normal conversation. Your speech is transcribed and sent as a message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the call">
    Tap the hang-up button or close the chat to end the voice session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Voice mode requires a supported browser and microphone access. If the voice button is unavailable, check your browser permissions.
</Note>

## Talk to Maya on WhatsApp

You can message Maya from WhatsApp instead of opening Kolmena. Kolmena runs one shared WhatsApp number. Each person who messages it reaches their own Maya, so your conversations stay private to your account.

### Connect WhatsApp

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Maya settings">
    Go to **Settings** > **Maya** and find the **WhatsApp** row. Click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open WhatsApp">
    In the **Connect WhatsApp** dialog, click **Open WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens a chat with the Kolmena number and a `connect …` message already filled in. If your browser doesn't open WhatsApp, use the **Open it here** link in the dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the message">
    Tap **Send** in WhatsApp. The link works for 30 minutes. If it expires, Maya replies with a message asking you to tap **Connect** again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Return to Kolmena and click **I sent it**. The WhatsApp row now shows **Connected**, the Kolmena number, and the last digits of your linked phone number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can link one phone per account. Linking a second phone replaces the first.

If you message the Kolmena number from a phone that isn't linked, Maya replies with instructions to open **Settings** > **Maya** > **WhatsApp** and tap **Connect**.

### How Maya replies on WhatsApp

- Replies are plain text with light formatting, such as bold and lists.
- WhatsApp replies have no buttons. When Maya asks you to choose, the options come as a numbered list. Reply with your choice.
- Long replies are split into several messages.
- You can send and receive files. If Maya produces a file WhatsApp doesn't accept, Maya names it in a note after the reply. Open Kolmena to get that file.
- Maya can message you on WhatsApp only within 24 hours of your last message. Results and reminders that arrive later aren't sent to WhatsApp.
- Group chats aren't supported. Message the Kolmena number directly.

### Availability

WhatsApp may not be available in every country. If the Kolmena number doesn't serve your phone's country, it replies that Kolmena on WhatsApp isn't available for your country yet and doesn't link your number. If WhatsApp isn't offered at all, the WhatsApp row in **Settings** > **Maya** shows **Not available on this deployment** and has no **Connect** button.

### Disconnect WhatsApp

In **Settings** > **Maya**, click the disconnect button on the WhatsApp row and confirm. Maya stops answering you on WhatsApp. You can connect again at any time.
