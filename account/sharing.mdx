Kolmena lets you share conversations and dashboards with others through public links and controlled access modes. You remain in control and can revoke access at any time.

## Share an agent or swarm chat

You can publish any one-on-one agent chat or swarm conversation to a read-only public link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation panel">
    While chatting with an agent or inside a swarm, open the details panel on the right side of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable public sharing">
    Find the **Public Chat** toggle and switch it on. Kolmena generates a unique share token and URL instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    Click **Copy Link** to copy the public URL to your clipboard. Anyone with the link can view the conversation in read-only mode.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Shared chats are static snapshots. Visitors cannot send messages or interact with your agents.
</Note>

### Revoke a chat share

To stop sharing, open the same details panel and toggle **Public Chat** off. The link becomes invalid immediately.

## Share a dashboard

Dashboards created by your agents can be shared with three access modes. Open a dashboard's **Manage Access** dialog from its card or detail view.

### Access modes

| Mode | Who can view |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Anyone with the link |
| **Private** | Only you |
| **Restricted** | People you explicitly invite |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an access mode">
    Select **Public**, **Private**, or **Restricted**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite viewers (Restricted only)">
    Enter an email address and click **Grant access**. The invited person receives access once they sign in. You can also copy the invite link and send it yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or deny requests">
    If someone without access opens a Restricted dashboard link, they can request access. You will see pending requests in the same dialog and can **Approve** or **Deny** them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Manage dashboard members

In the **Manage Access** dialog, the **Who can access** list shows every viewer, including the owner, invited viewers, and pending signups. Click **Remove** next to any member to revoke their access.

### App links

Every dashboard or app, whether an Instant App or a Full App, has one readable link in this format:

```text
/a/<id>
```

The `<id>` is built from the app's title plus a short random suffix, for example `/a/sales-dashboard-k3j5q2xa`. The link is set when the app is created and does not change if the title changes. When you ask your agent to change the app, it publishes a new version (v2, v3, and so on) at the same link.

Links in the old `/dashboard-access/<jobId>` format still work. Kolmena redirects them to the app's `/a/<id>` link.

If the app is Private, visitors see an access-denied message and can request access only if the owner allows it.

### Change access from the app's top bar

When you open your own app, a top bar shows the app title, the current version, and an access selector. Choose **Private** or **Anyone with the link** to change who can open it. If the app is set to Restricted in the **Manage Access** dialog, the selector also lists **Restricted**. Visitors see the current access mode as plain text instead of a selector.

<Tip>
  Marketplace copies of dashboards cannot be shared. The Manage Access option is hidden for those items.
</Tip>
