Your Kolmena account settings are where you control how you appear to others and how you sign in. Open **Settings** from the main navigation to reach the Profile, Billing, Maya, Secrets, Browsers, Memory, and Logs tabs.

## Profile

The Profile tab is the default landing page for Settings. It has two sections: Personal Information and Sign-In Methods.

### Personal Information

This section controls how you appear across Kolmena, including on marketplace listings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change your avatar">
    Click your profile picture and select **Change avatar**. Choose a JPG, PNG, GIF, or WEBP image up to 5 MB. To remove it, select **Remove avatar** from the same menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your display name">
    Click **Edit** next to your name, type a new display name, and press **Save** or hit Enter. Press Escape to cancel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Sign-In Methods

Kolmena supports signing in with Email, Google, SMS, and Discord. You can link multiple methods so you always have a backup way to access your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Link a new method">
    Find the method you want to add (for example, Google) and click **Link**. Follow the provider's prompt to connect the account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlink a method">
    Click the connected account row, then confirm **Unlink**. You must keep at least one sign-in method active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Linking or unlinking an account may briefly refresh your session so Kolmena recognizes the change.
</Tip>

## Maya

The Maya tab lets you choose which models Maya uses and connect Maya to messaging apps under **Channels**.

### Connect your own Telegram bot

You can give Maya its own Telegram bot. Your bot answers only you, because every message it handles runs your Maya with your tools, connected accounts, and credits.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a bot with BotFather">
    In Telegram, open @BotFather, create a new bot, and copy the bot token it gives you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the token to Kolmena">
    Go to **Settings > Maya > Channels**, click **Connect** on the Telegram row, paste the token into **Bot Token**, and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your Telegram account">
    Connecting a bot does not link your Telegram account. If your account isn't linked yet, Kolmena shows a **Link your Telegram account first** notice under the Telegram row. Click **Link Telegram account**, press **Start** in the Telegram chat that opens, then return to Kolmena and click **I pressed Start**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Until you link your Telegram account, your bot answers nobody, including you.
</Warning>

Your bot handles messages from other people as follows:

| Who sends the message | What your bot does |
| --- | --- |
| You, from a linked Telegram account | Maya answers. |
| Anyone else, in a private chat | The bot replies once: "This bot answers only its owner. If it is your bot, link your Telegram account in Kolmena: **Settings → Maya → Channels**." Maya does not run. |
| Anyone else, in a group | The bot stays silent. |

Linking your Telegram account also lets Maya answer you on the shared Kolmena bot.

## Other Settings Tabs

| Tab | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Billing** | View your plan, top up LLM credits, manage subscriptions. See [Plans and Billing](/account/plans-and-billing). |
| **Maya** | Choose which models Maya uses and connect messaging channels like Telegram or Discord. |
| **Secrets** | Store API keys and credentials that your agents can use securely. |
| **Browsers** | Manage connected browser sessions for web-based agent tasks. |
| **Memory** | Control what Kolmena remembers about you and how memory is shared across agents. |
| **Logs** | Search and filter system logs. See [Usage and Logs](/account/usage-and-logs). |
