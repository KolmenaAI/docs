title: "Create and Configure a Swarm of AI Agents"
sidebarTitle: "Create a Swarm"
description: "Build a swarm by naming it, writing a goal prompt, choosing a leader, and selecting at least two member agents from your roster or external sources."
---

You can create a swarm from the Swarms page in your workspace. A swarm needs a clear goal, a leader, and at least two members. Once saved, the swarm is ready to receive messages and delegate work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the create dialog">
    Go to **Swarms** in the sidebar, then click **New Swarm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the swarm">
    Enter a name that reflects the mission, such as "Weekly Report Team" or "Customer Support Escalation."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the goal prompt">
    Describe what the swarm should accomplish. The prompt should be at least 50 characters so the leader has enough context to plan and delegate. You can click **Improve Prompt** after adding agents to enrich the description with their capabilities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a leader">
    Choose who leads the swarm:

    - **Maya** — she orchestrates the swarm and chats with you directly.
    - **One of your agents** — the agent becomes the orchestrator and speaks for the swarm.

    The leader must be one of the selected members. You cannot assign an external agent as leader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Select at least two agents total. You can add:

    - **Your agents** from the Internal tab. Use the search box to filter by name.
    - **External agents** from the External tab. Paste the agent's base URL and verify it. Kolmena reads the agent card from `/.well-known/agent-card.json` under that URL.

    If you deselect the current leader, the crown moves to the first remaining selected agent automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the swarm">
    Click **Create Swarm**. Kolmena builds the team and takes you to the swarm chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for a strong swarm

- **Match skills to the goal.** A research swarm benefits from an analyst and a writer. A support swarm benefits from a triage agent and an escalation agent.
- **Keep the prompt focused.** One clear mission is easier to delegate than a broad list of unrelated tasks.
- **Start with your own agents.** External agents are useful for extending reach, but your own agents share your knowledge base and credentials.

<Note>
  A swarm needs at least two members and a leader. External agents count toward the member minimum, but only your own agents can lead.
</Note>
