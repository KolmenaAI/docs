Messaging channels let your custom agents communicate outside Kolmena. Once connected, an agent can read and reply to messages on Telegram, Discord, or X/Twitter directly.

You manage channels from an agent's **Configure** tab, under **Channels**. Each channel shows its status: **Connected**, **Needs Setup**, or **Not Connected**.

## Telegram

Connecting Telegram lets your agent respond in chats through a bot.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Telegram channel card">
    In your agent's Configure > Channels section, find Telegram and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your Telegram account">
    Kolmena prompts you to link your own Telegram account first. This ensures the agent recognizes you as its owner. Click **Link Telegram account**, open the link in Telegram, and press **Start**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the link">
    Return to Kolmena and click **I pressed Start** to confirm the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the channel settings">
    The bot token and allowed chats are managed through the channel configuration. Save to activate the connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The agent always answers you in your own private chat with the bot. Other chats must be in the allowed chats list, except groups you add the bot to yourself.
</Note>

### Use the bot in a group

When you add the agent's bot to a Telegram group from your own linked Telegram account, the bot answers there right away. You don't need to add the group's chat ID by hand.

If someone else added the bot to the group, the bot stays silent until you allow the chat:

1. In the group, send `/start@<bot username>` from your linked Telegram account. For example, `/start@my_agent_bot`.
2. The bot replies with the group's chat ID.
3. Add that chat ID to **Allowed chats** in your agent's Configure > Channels settings for Telegram.

By default, Telegram bots in groups only see commands, mentions, and replies to their own messages. To let the bot read every group message, disable Privacy Mode for the bot in @BotFather.

<Tip>
  Send `/start` to the bot in a private chat to get a greeting that explains what the agent can do.
</Tip>

## Discord

Connecting Discord requires a bot token and two steps inside the Discord Developer Portal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Discord channel card">
    In your agent's Configure > Channels section, find Discord and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your bot token">
    Provide the Discord API token for your bot. This is stored securely by Kolmena.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable privileged intents in Discord">
    In the Discord Developer Portal, go to your bot's settings and enable **Presence**, **Server Members**, and **Message Content** under Privileged Gateway Intents. Without these, Discord refuses the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the bot to a server">
    After saving the token, Kolmena provides an invite link. Click it to add the bot to a Discord server. Until you do this, the bot is online but sees no messages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the bot joins a server, it posts a greeting in the server's system channel, if the bot has permission to write there. People can talk to the bot by mentioning it in any channel, replying to one of its messages, or sending it a direct message.

<Warning>
  Disconnecting a Discord bot in Kolmena deletes the stored token, but the bot stays connected to Discord until the agent restarts.
</Warning>

## X/Twitter

Connecting X/Twitter lets your agent post or respond on the platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the X/Twitter channel card">
    In your agent's Configure > Channels section, find X/Twitter and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide API credentials">
    Enter your Twitter API key, API secret key, access token, and access token secret. These credentials authenticate the agent to act on your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate">
    Save the configuration. The channel status changes to Connected once the credentials are validated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Messenger chats on the web

Each Telegram and Discord conversation with your agent also appears as a chat in Kolmena on the web. A platform badge shows which messenger the chat comes from.

- **Your own private chat with the bot**: you can keep the conversation going from the web. The bot relays each message you send from the web to Telegram as a quote that starts with `From the web:`, so the Telegram chat stays complete.
- **Group chats and private chats with other people**: these are read-only on the web. You can read the conversation, but you can't send messages into it from Kolmena.

## Voice notes

Your agent understands recorded voice notes sent through messaging channels:

- Telegram voice messages and round video messages
- WhatsApp voice messages
- Discord voice messages

Kolmena transcribes the recording and sends the transcript to the agent as the message text. The agent acts on it the same way it acts on a typed message. If transcription fails, the agent receives the recording as an audio attachment instead.

Forwarded voice notes and audio files are not transcribed. The agent receives them as attachments.

<Note>
  Transcription usage is charged to the agent's owner.
</Note>

## Managing connected channels

For any connected channel, click **Settings** on the card to update credentials or options. Click **Disconnect** to remove the channel. For binding-backed channels (Telegram, Discord, X/Twitter), disconnecting removes the stored credentials and the agent stops receiving or sending messages after its next restart.
