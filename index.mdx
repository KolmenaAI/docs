Kolmena is your workspace for AI agents. You chat with Maya, your personal AI assistant, who helps you create custom agents, assemble them into swarms (teams of agents), schedule recurring work, connect third-party apps, and manage everything from one place. You can also discover and install agents from the marketplace, or publish your own to earn revenue.
