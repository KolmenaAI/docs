Selling on the Kolmena Marketplace lets you share your agents and swarms with the community and earn revenue. You control the pricing, offer free trials, and publish updates whenever you improve your agent. This page walks you through creating a listing, managing it, and setting up payouts.

## Before you create a listing

To list an agent, it must have a published live version. Swarms must be created and saved in your workspace. Rented agents and swarm members cannot be listed.

## Create a listing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the listing wizard">
    In the Marketplace, click **Create listing**. The wizard opens with six steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set identity">
    Choose whether you are listing an **Agent** or a **Swarm**. Select the source from your workspace, then add a public title and a short tagline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set pricing">
    Pick a pricing model:

    - **Subscription** - Charge a monthly recurring fee.
    - **One-time** - Charge a single purchase price.
    - **Free** - Let users install at no cost.

    For paid listings, enter the price amount and choose a currency: USD, EUR, or GBP. You can also offer a free trial of up to 30 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose categories">
    Select at least one category and up to three. Categories help buyers discover your listing when they filter the Marketplace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the about page">
    Add a sales pitch that explains what your agent or swarm does and why it is valuable. Upload up to 8 screenshots with captions to showcase the experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bundle dashboards (optional)">
    If your agent or swarm has dashboards, you can bundle them into the listing. Each bundled dashboard needs a short description for buyers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    Preview how your card will look in the Marketplace. When you are ready, click **Create** to publish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage your listings

After publishing, your listing appears in **My listings**. From there you can:

- Edit the title, tagline, sales pitch, screenshots, categories, and pricing.
- Publish updates when you release a new version of the underlying agent or swarm. Renters see a **Pull update** button to apply the latest changes.
- Track installs and engagement over time.

## Set up payouts with Stripe Connect

To receive earnings from paid listings, you must connect a Stripe Connect account:

1. Go to your account settings and open the payouts section.
2. Complete the Stripe Connect onboarding flow. This includes identity verification and bank account details.
3. Once payouts are enabled, revenue from your paid listings is transferred to your connected account automatically.

<Note>
  Payouts are only sent after your Stripe Connect account is fully onboarded and payouts are enabled. Until then, earnings are held and transferred once the account is ready.
</Note>

## Pricing and fees

You set the price. Kolmena takes a platform fee from each transaction, and the remainder is paid out to your Stripe Connect account. Free listings incur no charges.
