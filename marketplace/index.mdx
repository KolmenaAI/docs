The Kolmena Marketplace is where you discover, install, and manage AI agents and swarms built by the community. Whether you need a research assistant, a trading bot, or a full team of agents, you can browse listings, try them with free trials, and add them to your workspace in seconds.

## What you can find

Listings on the Marketplace fall into two types:

- **Agents** - Single AI agents with a specific skill set, such as market analysis, content writing, or customer support.
- **Swarms** - Teams of agents that work together. A swarm may have a leader and multiple members, each with a dedicated role.

## Browse and filter

When you open the Marketplace, you see featured listings and a search bar. Use the filters to narrow results by category. Available categories include:

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Trading | Market analysis, portfolio management |
| Research | Data gathering, report generation |
| Productivity | Task automation, scheduling |
| Support | Customer service, helpdesk |
| Marketing | Campaign management, SEO |
| Finance | Accounting, expense tracking |
| Development | Code review, debugging |
| Design | Image generation, UI feedback |
| Writing | Copywriting, editing |
| Data Analytics | Visualization, trend analysis |
| Automation | Workflow orchestration |
| Legal | Contract review, compliance |
| Education | Tutoring, curriculum planning |
| Health | Wellness tracking, meal planning |
| Lifestyle | Travel planning, recommendations |

## Pricing models

Each listing shows its price clearly on the card. Creators can choose from three models:

- **Free** - Install at no cost.
- **One-time purchase** - Pay once and keep the agent or swarm forever.
- **Subscription** - Pay a monthly fee. Subscriptions may include a free trial period set by the creator.

## Reviews and ratings

Every listing has a review section. After you install and use a listing, you can leave a star rating and a short review to help others decide. You can also edit or delete your review later.

## Rentals and ownership

When you install a paid listing, Kolmena creates a private copy in your workspace. For agents, you receive an API key that lets you call the agent from outside Kolmena. For swarms, you get a copy of the entire team. You can manage or cancel subscriptions at any time from your agent or swarm detail page.
